## Description

Welcome to the boardwalk. Roll balls up a bright, shiny arcade ramp into glowing rings. Every ring shows a slot machine picture, and matching pictures make your score climb.

Pull the lever to set your targets, flick your thumb to throw, and beat the score for each round. Spend your tickets at the prize counter on charms and special balls, and save your winnings for the prize shelf.

Then there are the hosts. Every few games a casino host sits down with you, and everything's negotiable. Talk to them in your own words, with AI playing the host if you allow it. Ask nicely for more nights, tickets and credit. Push too hard and they'll walk.

## App Review notes

No account or login is needed.

All play uses in-game tickets and credit. There is no real money, no purchases, and no way to cash anything out.

The first launch opens with a short scene at the casino door, then a chat with a host named Sal. Before anything typed leaves the phone, a card asks permission and says it goes to a third-party AI model. The privacy policy names the provider, currently DeepSeek. Choosing no keeps everything on the device and the host uses built-in replies. The choice can be changed at the top of the host's screen. Type anything, or tap DEAL. A short practice game follows, and the Guide button stays available during play.

The hosts accept small talk but are told to avoid politics, religion, advice, adult content and real gambling, and every reply is checked on the device before it's shown. Phone numbers, emails and card numbers are removed before anything is sent. Mentions of self-harm get a reply with the 988 line.

The AI replies go through our own server, which holds the API key. Each reply can only change the deal within limits enforced in the app.

To throw: put your thumb on the lane and flick straight up. Tap SPIN or the lever first to set the frame's targets.
