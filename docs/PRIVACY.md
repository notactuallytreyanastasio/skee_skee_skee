# Privacy Policy: Skee Skee Skee

Last updated: November 4, 2026

Skee Skee Skee has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
The one thing that ever leaves your phone is what you choose to type to a
casino host, and only after you say yes.

## AI chat with the hosts

The casino hosts can be played by an AI. The first time you sit down with one,
the game asks your permission. If you say yes:

- **What is sent:** what you type at the host's table, the conversation so far,
  and the details of the deal being discussed. Your name, contacts and location
  are not sent, and the game doesn't know them.
- **Who receives it:** our relay server, which passes it to a third-party AI
  model, currently DeepSeek, to write the host's reply. DeepSeek sees our
  server's address, not yours.
- **What's held back:** the game checks each line first. Phone numbers, email
  addresses, card numbers and similar details are removed on your phone and
  never sent.
- **What we keep:** our server doesn't store or log your chats. It holds your
  network address briefly in memory to limit how often one address can call it,
  then drops it.
- **Please don't type anything private.**

If you say no, nothing you type leaves your phone, and the hosts use simpler
built-in replies. You can change your answer at any time at the top of the
host's screen. The built-in replies are also used whenever you're offline.

## What stays on your phone

Your game progress, winnings and prizes are saved on your device and included
in your device backups if you use them. Deleting the app deletes them.

## No real money

The game has no purchases and no real-money gambling. Tickets, credit and
winnings have no cash value.
